Features

A workout tracker that does what the spreadsheet could not

Every feature works the same on your phone and on a laptop. Here is each one on both.

Today

See your whole day on one screen: gym time, streak and every number against its goal.

You can:

  • See gym time from your finished sessions, or type your own
  • Track steps, calories, weight and walk time against your goals
  • Read your heart rate zones with their bpm ranges
  • Check the week totals: sessions, gym minutes, calories, steps

Day view and date picker

Open any earlier day and see it as it was.

You can:

  • Step back and forward one day with the arrows
  • Jump to any date with the date picker
  • Open a day from the streak heat map
  • Fix a day you logged wrong

Log the day

Fill in the 8 columns of the old spreadsheet in one form, with no keyboard for times.

You can:

  • Set walk, gym and swim time with preset chips and −15 s / +15 s
  • Enter heart rate, weight, calories and steps
  • Add a note to the day
  • See a Saved message after every save

Stats and charts

Day, Week and Month charts that show whether you are getting stronger, including the volume and one-rep max trends you need for progressive overload.

You can:

  • Follow your weight with a 7-day average line
  • See volume load, one-rep max and personal records
  • Compare muscle groups and the gaps between sessions
  • Reorder and hide the chart cards

Live workout

Track sets and reps as you lift, with a session timer and a rest countdown.

You can:

  • Pick an exercise by search, muscle or your recent list
  • Log reps and weight per set, and untick with undo
  • Rest with a countdown timer
  • See the volume add up while you train

Coach plans

Follow a coach plan one exercise at a time.

You can:

  • Let the app suggest the plan you did least recently
  • Join 2 plans on a day you missed
  • See start and end photos, the how-to and a YouTube tutorial link
  • Go Next, Back or Skip at your own pace

Muscle map

See which muscles an exercise works before you lift.

You can:

  • See the main muscles and the helpers on a front and back body map
  • Read the muscle names as chips under the map
  • Switch between How to and Muscles on the step photo

Session done

Finish a plan and see what you did.

You can:

  • See gym time, volume and sets for the session
  • See each step as done, skipped or not done
  • Spot your new records
  • Go back to Workouts in one tap

Saved videos

Keep the Instagram Reels and YouTube videos you want to try, in your own categories.

You can:

  • Save a link with a title, a category, tags and a priority
  • See the high priority videos first
  • Filter by category and search titles and tags
  • Tap a card to open the video in its own app

CSV import

Bring your old spreadsheet with you, from Profile › Import the old sheet, when the import is turned on for the app.

You can:

  • Import times written as 15.54, 1:03:13 or 01.44.50
  • Fix every unclear row on a review screen before anything is saved
  • Export every table as CSV in one zip

Profile and goals

Set your goals once and every chart and card uses them.

You can:

  • Set goals for steps, calories, gym time, walk time, sessions per week and weight
  • Give your birth year for the heart rate zones
  • See the sync state and the error list
  • Sign out safely: unsynced saves are pushed first

You can:

  • Log in flight mode, in a basement gym, anywhere
  • See where your data is from the chip on every data screen
  • Trust that a retry never makes a duplicate row

How offline sync works

An offline workout tracker: your phone first, the server when it can

The save goes to the device first. When you tap Save, the app writes the row to a database inside your browser and is done at once. No spinner, no lost set when the gym has no signal.

An outbox keeps the list. The same save also adds an entry to an outbox, in one step: never one without the other. Each row gets its own id on the device, so a retry can never make a second copy.

The sync pushes when online. The outbox drains when the app starts, the moment the network comes back, and every 30 seconds while you are online. A delete is a mark, not a removal, so it syncs too.

Each account sees only its own rows. Every table on the server has a rule: a row is visible only to the account that owns it.

  1. 1
    SaveYou log a set. The screen updates at once.
  2. 2
    Device databaseThe row and its outbox entry, in one step.
  3. 3
    OutboxWaits while you are offline. Retries after a failure.
  4. 4
    ServerStores the row under your account, and only yours.

Install the app

Install the PWA on iPhone, Android or laptop in a minute

No app store and no download. Open the app in your browser, make an account, then add it to your Home Screen or your Dock. Pick your device:

iPhone and iPad

Use Safari. Other browsers on iPhone and iPad cannot add an app to the Home Screen.

  1. Open the app

    Go to gym-tracker-ten-gamma.vercel.app.

  2. Create an account

    Tap Create an account and enter your name, email and a password of 8 or more characters. Or sign in if you have one.

  3. Tap Share

    Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow. On iPhone it is in the bottom bar, on iPad at the top right.

  4. Add to Home Screen

    Scroll the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.

  5. Tap Add

    Tap Add at the top right. The Gym Tracker icon is now on your Home Screen.

Android

Use Chrome.

  1. Open the app

    Go to gym-tracker-ten-gamma.vercel.app.

  2. Create an account

    Tap Create an account and enter your name, email and a password of 8 or more characters. Or sign in if you have one.

  3. Open the menu

    Tap ⋮, the 3 dots at the top right of Chrome.

  4. Install

    Tap Install app. On some phones it says Add to Home screen.

  5. Confirm

    Tap Install. The icon is now on your Home screen and in your app list.

Laptop: Chrome or Edge

Use Chrome or Microsoft Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS.

  1. Open the app

    Go to gym-tracker-ten-gamma.vercel.app.

  2. Create an account

    Tap Create an account and enter your name, email and a password of 8 or more characters. Or sign in if you have one.

  3. Find the install button

    Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, a small screen with a down arrow.

  4. Or use the menu

    In Chrome: ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install Gym Tracker. In Edge: … → Apps → Install Gym Tracker.

  5. Click Install

    Gym Tracker opens in its own window, and you can pin it to the taskbar or the Dock.

Mac: Safari

Use Safari 17 or newer, on macOS Sonoma or later.

  1. Open the app

    Go to gym-tracker-ten-gamma.vercel.app.

  2. Create an account

    Tap Create an account and enter your name, email and a password of 8 or more characters. Or sign in if you have one.

  3. Open the File menu

    In the menu bar, click File.

  4. Add to Dock

    Click Add to Dock.

  5. Click Add

    Click Add. Gym Tracker is now in your Dock and opens in its own window.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Gym Tracker free?

Yes. It is free, with no ads, and the code is open source on GitHub. Anyone can create an account.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. Every save lands on your device first, so you can log sets in a gym with no network. The app syncs your saves by itself when you are back online.

How do I install it on iPhone?

Open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add. It opens full screen from its own icon. The same steps work on iPad.

How do I install it on Android?

Open the app in Chrome, tap the 3-dot menu, then Install app (on some phones: Add to Home screen), then Install.

Can I use it on my phone and my laptop with one account?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on both. Your days, workouts and saved videos sync between them.

Is my data private?

Each account sees only its own rows. Every table on the server has a Row Level Security rule that shows a row only to the account that owns it.
