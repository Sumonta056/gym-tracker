## Do

1. Add 5 plates to `docs/design/prototype/index.html`, numbered after the last plate:
   - Choose a plan · `/workouts`: the hero, the 4 plan cards with a chip-size muscle
     map, the Start bar, then the free session launcher and the history from the fix
     track.
   - Plan preview · `/workouts/[planId]`, with a watch pill on 1 row.
   - Guided step · `/workout`, plan mode, in 2 states side by side: the frame on Play,
     and the frame on Muscles.
   - Session done · `/workout`, after Finish.
   - Guided step at 1440 px, in the laptop section, with the 3-cell image row.
2. Reuse the prototype classes: `.frame`, `.card`, `.hero`, `.setrow`, `.cell`, `.tick`,
   `.btn`, `.chip`, `.tabbar`. Add 5 classes only: `.stepcard`, `.stepbar`, `.plancard`,
   `.stepframe` with its segmented control, and `.musclemap`.
3. The guided step plate has no `.tabbar`. Its header carries the back control, the
   title and the elapsed time.
4. Use 4 real images from `docs/data/coach-images/` in the plates, as base64. The
   prototype stays one self-contained file. Draw the muscle map from
   `docs/data/body-polygons.json`, inline, for Flat Dumbbell Press.
5. In `docs/plan/03-phase-3.md` and `docs/plan/phase-3/index.md`, check every link
   resolves. In `CLAUDE.md`, add Phase 3 to the **Phases** paragraph.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The 5 plates match the mockup: the layout, the copy and the order of parts.
- [ ] Every colour in the new plates comes from a `--color-*` token.
- [ ] Every button in the new plates has an explicit `type`.
- [ ] Every image has an alt text that names the exercise and the position.
- [ ] The progress bars carry a text label, not colour only.
- [ ] The muscle map plate names the muscles as text beside the figures.
- [ ] `pnpm design:check` passes.
- [ ] No link in the Phase 3 docs points at a missing file.
- [ ] The owner opened the prototype and accepted the plates.
