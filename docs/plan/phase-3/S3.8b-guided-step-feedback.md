## The decisions

1. **Any order.** The header line `Step 2 of 9 ▾` is a button. It opens a step list
   sheet with every exercise of the session and a status mark: done ✓, now ●, skipped
   –, not started ○. The mark is a shape and a word, never colour alone. A tap moves
   the cursor to that step. Next and Back still move in order.
2. **Finish at any time.** A `Finish` button in the header on every step, next to the
   elapsed time, 44 px. With steps not done, it asks to confirm and names how many are
   not done. The summary shows which steps were not done. The footer keeps Skip and
   Next; the last step keeps Finish session and no Skip.
3. **The weight is a dropdown.** Per equipment, plus `Other…` to type a value:
   - Dumbbell: 2.5 to 50 kg in 2.5 kg steps.
   - Cable and machine: 5 to 100 kg in 5 kg steps.
   - Barbell: 20 to 150 kg in 2.5 kg steps.
   - Body weight keeps the fixed `Body` cell.
     The value in storage stays metric. The imperial setting stays display only.
4. **2 view buttons.** `How to` (was Play: start and end in turn) and `Muscles`. Start
   and End go away. `How to` is the default.
5. **The target is plain text.** `3 × 10–12` in muted text under the name. No pill.
6. **The video link reads `▶ Tutorial on YouTube`**, on the step card and on the plan
   preview. It stays an `<a>` with `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener"`, 44 px.
7. **A compact card.** The image is 16:9, not 3:2. The 2 view buttons sit on the image.
   Then the name, the target, the muscle chips and the YouTube pill. About 30 % less
   height than 3.8.

## Decisions from the demo

The owner reviewed the part 1 demo, https://claude.ai/artifact/D4dbEj8jaio3vRVyCjeP5Z,
on 2026-10-20. The plates 17, 18, 18b, 18c, 18d and 18e are the contract.

8. **The image is full bleed.** It touches the card's left, right and top edges, its
   top corners follow the 20 px card radius, and it stays 16:9.
9. **The header is 1 row of 3 items.** Back; the title with 1 button under it that
   reads `Step 2 of 9 · 12:41 ▾` and opens the step list; `Finish`. The `Elapsed`
   label goes away. The button's name says the step and the elapsed time.
10. **A step is done after 1 ticked set.** 4 marks only: Done, Now, Skipped, Not
    started.
11. **The Finish count includes the current step** when it has no ticked set.
12. **The one-line description leaves the card.**
13. **A stored weight off the list** joins the list in order, already selected.
14. **The weight control is a native `<select>`.** Plate 18d draws the list only to
    show the values.
15. **`Last time 15 kg × 10` stays** as plain muted text after the target.
