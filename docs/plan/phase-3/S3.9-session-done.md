2. The Phase 2 gym time offer from step 2.6.

## State before the step

Plan mode runs 1 plan at 390 px. Finish uses the Phase 2 end screen. The laptop shows
the phone layout, wider.

## Goal

The user sees what the session did, runs 2 plans in one go, and uses the same flow on
a laptop.

## Do

1. The done screen, after `Finish session`:
   - The header `Session done` and the plan title.
   - The hero: `Gym time`, the time, and `Volume 3,420 kg · 25 sets · 8 of 9 steps`,
     from `sessionSummary`.
   - The step card: 1 row per step, the name, then `✓ 3 × 12 kg` or `Skipped`. The
     skipped rows carry the word, not color only.
   - The Phase 2 gym time offer, unchanged.
   - `Back to Workouts`.
2. With every step skipped, the hero reads `0 of 9 steps`, and the gym time offer does
   not show.
3. 2 plans: the session runs `combinePlans(plan_ids)`. The header shows
   `Shoulders + Legs`, and `Step 6 of 8 · Legs`. The progress bar has 8 bars.
4. At 1024 px and up, plan mode takes 2 columns inside the 1100 px frame:
   - Left, 360 px: the step list. Each row: a thumbnail, the name, the status. The
     current row has the accent border. A row is a `<button>` that moves the cursor
     to that step.
   - Right: the image card, larger. Under it, the set rows beside a 300 px column with
     the rest timer, `Next step: <name>` and `Skip this step`.
   - The header row: `Step 2 of 9 · Chest`, the title, the elapsed time and
     `End session`.
5. At 640 to 1023 px, keep the phone layout, centred, with the 28 px gutters.
