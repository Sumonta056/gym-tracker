## State before the step

The lifting charts exist. Nothing shows the balance of the week or the gaps.

## Goal

The user sees which muscle group falls behind and how regular the sessions are.

## Do

1. Muscle group balance: a stacked bar with a legend. When one group falls below 15
   percent of the weekly volume, show a warning with text and a shape, in
   `--color-warn`.
2. The session gap histogram: bars for a gap of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 or more days.
3. Calories per gym minute: a line, from the daily rows.
4. The same chart rules as step 2.8a.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each chart renders its empty state before any session exists.
- [ ] Each chart renders with one session, with no lone dot.
- [ ] The balance warning appears below the threshold and not above it.
- [ ] The warning is readable as text, not by color only.
- [ ] The histogram shows the gaps 1, 3, 3, 3, 1 for the 2 to 13 September dates.
- [ ] Every chart follows `charts.md`.
