- [ ] The streak number equals the value from `lib/metrics/streak`.
- [ ] The gym time renders through `formatDuration`. No component parses `mm:ss` by
      hand.
- [ ] The sync chip reads its state from `useSyncStatus`.
- [ ] The cards form one column under 640 px, two columns from 640 px, and three
      columns at 1024 px and up.
- [ ] The heart rate zone bar carries a text alternative. The same numbers are
      readable outside the bar.
- [ ] No raw hex appears in any new file. The design check proves this.
