Heart rate zones 21m in cardio

Heart rate zones

Warm, under 114 bpm
Easy movement below 60 % of your max, such as a warm-up or the rest between sets.
Fat burn, 114–132 bpm
A steady effort from 60 to 70 % of your max that you could keep up for a long time.
Cardio, 133–161 bpm
Hard work from 70 to 85 % of your max that builds your heart and lung fitness.
Peak, 162+ bpm
An all-out effort above 85 % of your max that you can hold only for short bursts.

The app spreads your gym time between your average and your peak heart rate, so the split is a model, not a measurement.

Your max heart rate is the value you set in Profile. Without one, it is 220 minus your age, from your birth year.

What the choices do
  • Overwrite replaces a day with its sheet row. A blank sheet cell clears that field. The note of the day stays.
  • Merge fills only the empty fields of a day and keeps the rest. A Merge that fills nothing writes nothing and counts as skipped.
