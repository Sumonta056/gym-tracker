Gym Tracker — the visual contract

Thirteen plates at 390 px, and the live workout at 1440 px below them. Every colour is a token declared once on :root. This file imports no project code, so it cannot drift by accident.

Plates 00 to 06 are built from the written specification and the token table only. Plates 07 to 11 and the 1440 px plate take their look from the canvas mockup and their behaviour from the Phase 2 plan.
